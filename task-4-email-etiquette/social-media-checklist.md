# Social Media Do’s and Don’ts

## ✅ Do’s
- Think before posting anything online
- Verify information before sharing
- Maintain a respectful and polite tone
- Protect your personal information
- Use social media for learning and networking

## ❌ Don’ts
- Do not share sensitive information (OTP, passwords, address)
- Do not spread misinformation or unverified news
- Do not engage in online harassment or arguments
- Do not post impulsively when emotional
- Do not overshare personal life details publicly
