# Phishing Awareness Visual Novel

As part of my exploration of phishing awareness, I previously created a short interactive visual novel.

## Concept
The player takes on the role of a new security analyst on their first day. A senior analyst, Mr. Rao, guides them through identifying phishing emails.

## Gameplay
- Players analyze 5 email scenarios
- Make decisions based on given options
- Receive different endings (good, neutral, bad)
- Feedback is provided explaining each choice

## Purpose
The goal of this project was to make phishing awareness more engaging and practical by simulating real-life decision-making instead of just explaining concepts.
