# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

- Always verify the sender before clicking on links in emails or messages
- Do not share OTPs, passwords, or personal details with anyone
- Check URLs carefully for spelling errors or suspicious domains
- Avoid clicking on urgent or “too good to be true” offers
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on important accounts
- Do not use public Wi-Fi for financial transactions

## UPI / Financial Safety
- Never approve unknown payment requests on UPI apps
- Double-check receiver details before sending money

## Reporting
- Report cyber fraud at: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in
- Call Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
